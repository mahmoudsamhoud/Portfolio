From laundry loads to a shaft assembly.

A third-year mechanical engineering university project at Cairo University, completed in December 2020 by a group of four students. The work combines load estimation, shaft sizing, belt and bearing selection, and SOLIDWORKS assembly modeling.

The documentation records the complete supplied subsystem and separates the original academic calculations from the later CAD audit.

The report names a Beko Arcelic BLW090P8L32Y motor and assumes 220 V, single-phase AC, 50-60 Hz. These are historical report claims; a supplier data sheet was not supplied. Motor and belt are outside the native assembly.

Loading and shaft design.

The report estimates 1.867 kg for the open-cylinder drum, 2.65 N m gravity-related unbalance torque, 4.7 N m acceleration torque and 7.35 N m peak torque. Its worst-case radial load is 1971.48 N, giving reported bearing reactions of 5855.29 N and 3883.8 N using a 100 mm support spacing and 197 mm overhang.

The preliminary calculation selects a 30 mm shaft. The detailed fatigue calculation uses a 36 mm section, Kf = 2.1645, Kfs = 1.8928, corrected endurance limit 147 MPa and reported Goodman factor 3.414. These are retained as academic results; the native CAD has different stepped dimensions.

Belt selection uses a 7:1 ratio, 90/630 mm sheaves and one A-section belt. The belt length and center-distance arithmetic is inconsistent, so the original selection is not presented as verified.

A traceable material assignment.

Missing materials were assigned in the independent project copy. Report designations are retained for the shaft (St50), key (St37), housing (EN-GJL-300) and inner drum (201 stainless steel). Bearing steel, carbon steel sleeves and locking parts, an aluminium pulley and hub, a 304 stainless outer drum and an NBR seal are proposed baselines.

The project database stores nominal densities only. Aluminium grade, elastomer formulation, heat treatment, corrosion compatibility and mechanical properties must be specified before manufacturing or simulation. A material label is not a supplier certificate.

SKF describes 100Cr6 as a common bearing-ring steel family in its components and materials reference. This supports a generic bearing-steel assignment, not certification of the imported parts.

How the subsystem comes together.

  1. The shaft connects the pulley-side drive to the drum hub.
  2. Two bearing assemblies locate the shaft in the housing; sleeves establish axial spacing.
  3. The pulley, key, locknut and locking washer form the drive-side connection.
  4. The hub, seal and drum components complete the modeled subsystem.

This explains the saved CAD relationships. The original mates include 13 concentric, 15 coincident and one distance mate. Keyway and thread discrepancies must be resolved before a physical assembly procedure can be approved.

Engineering review.

Belt length

At C=700 mm, the report equation gives 2635.12 mm, not 2495 mm. For L=2474 mm, the physical root is approximately C=611.95 mm, not 765.5 mm. The claimed D<C condition then fails. Re-select belt length and center distance together.

CAD / calculation scale

CAD pulley outside diameter is 430 mm, versus a reported 630 mm design diameter. CAD inner-drum envelope is 502 mm diameter x 402.5 mm, versus assumed 300 x 200 mm. Outside diameter is not necessarily pitch diameter, but these differences require a consistent design basis.

Bearing spacing

Bearing-subassembly origins differ by 79.51 mm axially; the report assumes 100 mm support spacing. Verify actual bearing load-center positions before recalculating reactions: component origins alone are not bearing centers.

Key and keyways

Native key: 16 x 12 x 18 mm; report: 16 x 10 x 18 mm. The native shaft has two 8 mm wide slots. Reconcile the key, pulley and shaft interfaces before manufacturing.

Shaft fatigue

The report analyzes a 36 mm critical diameter and 40 mm step; native CAD has 55, 50, 40 and 35 mm steps. Mean and alternating bending must be checked in the rotating material frame, treating unbalance and gravity separately.

Duty and torque

The 4.7 N m inertia torque applies during the assumed 3-second acceleration, not continuous steady-speed operation. A cycle history is required for fatigue and thermal conclusions.

Key stress arithmetic

For the report dimensions, shear=267.27/(16 x 18)=0.928 MPa. Bearing stress=267.27/(5 x 18)=2.970 MPa, not 1.85 MPa. These are arithmetic checks under the original simplified loading.

Model scope

The supplied subsystem has 14 main components, including two purchased bearing subassemblies. Motor, belt, supporting chassis and housing mounting fasteners are outside its scope. The presentation does not demonstrate an interference solve or operating motion simulation.
